Battlepass

A progression-based reward system that drove sustained engagement and monetization in FarmVille: Country Escape.

A progression-based mechanic bringing consistent motivation and value to daily play

This is an abridged process presentation due to NDA requirements.

FarmVille: Country Escape is one of Zynga's most popular mobile farming games with over 400,000 daily players. To advance engagement and introduce a new monetization layer, the team launched the Battle Pass — a seasonal progression system that gave players structured goals and meaningful rewards. I led the UX design for the feature end-to-end, from defining player journeys to Unity implementation and live iterations.

Objective

Design a system for Consistent Engagement

While events drove short bursts of play, there was no system encouraging consistent long-term engagement. Players lacked ongoing goals and incentives beyond short-term rewards.

The Battle Pass was designed to provide structured progression and meaningful rewards for players.

Research

Understanding player behavior and needs

I studied battle passes across shooters, RPGs, and strategy games to extract design principles that could work for a casual farming simulation, understanding what made them engaging and sustainable.

Battle Pass System

Task System Design

Daily Tasks: Expired in 24 hours to drive daily engagement across game features and encourage consistent player retention.

General Tasks: Unlocked every 4 days, providing longer-term progression goals and sustained engagement throughout the season.

Task System

Token System

Token System: Task completion awarded tokens that accumulated to unlock rewards, creating a clear progression pathway.

Reward Collection: Players could check and claim unlocked rewards in the dedicated rewards tab, providing immediate gratification and clear value communication.

Reward Structure

Free Track: Base rewards for all players.

Premium Track: Exclusive rewards for pass purchasers.

Endless Rewards: Introduced after milestone 30 to keep late-stage players engaged.

Reward Structure

Perks Design

Premium Pass included gameplay perks such as:

  • +20% Barn Space
  • Faster Crafting & Foraging
  • "Water Everything at Once" shortcut
  • Seasonal perks like "Divine Shower" (Christmas theme)

These perks improved the daily loop without breaking balance.

Outcome

The Battle Pass feature successfully launched and became a core retention mechanic for FarmVille: Country Escape, providing players with structured progression and meaningful rewards. The implementation exceeded expectations across key performance indicators, demonstrating strong player adoption and sustained engagement throughout the season.
